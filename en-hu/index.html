What Does "Paid Within 24 Hours" Actually Mean?

The ad shows a single number, but behind it are four separate steps, each running on its own clock.

  1. Withdrawal request. You enter the amount in the cashier and hit the button. The funds are reserved but still sit with the casino.
  2. Casino approval. The finance team, or an automated system, checks your account, bonus status, and previous transactions. In our tests, this took anywhere from 4 minutes to 38 hours.
  3. Handover to the payment provider. The casino sends the transfer to Skrill, the card issuer, or the blockchain.
  4. Funds credited. The money shows up in your wallet or bank account. At this point it's down to the provider and your bank.

When a site advertises casino payouts within 24 hours, the promise usually covers only the first two or three steps. None of them commit to a timeframe for step four, because they have no control over it.

So "up to 24 hours" is really an upper estimate for the casino's own process. A Friday-evening request, an expired ID card, or a card withdrawal can easily stretch it to two or three days.

Does the 24 Hours Start at the Request or at Approval?

It depends on the site. V.Vegas's FAQ clearly counts from approval, while BigClash's table only lists processing time, which covers the stage after approval.

That's why we always logged two timestamps: when we made the request and when the money arrived. If 30 hours passed between them, we didn't count it among casino payouts within 24 hours, even if the post-approval part took just 3 hours.

Why Do Casinos Advertise "Fast Withdrawals" When the Money Arrives Later?

From the casino's point of view, "fast" usually refers to internal processing, meaning how quickly the finance team gets to your request. The fact that Visa may then hold the transaction for two business days never makes it into the ad.

There's another reason, too. Almost everywhere, your first withdrawal is checked manually, while later ones are often approved automatically. On one of our test accounts, the first request took 26 hours. The second, to the same Skrill account, took 2 hours 15 minutes.

If your money hasn't arrived, check these in order:

  1. The request status in the cashier: "approved", "processing", or "sent".
  2. Your inbox, including the spam folder, since requests for extra documents often end up there.
  3. Your casino account, where a new request may have appeared under the KYC tab.
  4. The withdrawal method: did you use the same wallet or card you deposited with?
  5. Your bank account or wallet history, including pending transactions.
  6. The daily and weekly limits, because only the first part of a split payout arrives at once.
  7. Your KYC status, since an expired document can stop the process even after approval.

If the status says "sent" and the money still hasn't arrived after 24 hours, contact your bank or wallet provider with the transaction ID.

Small Details That Can Derail a Fast Payout

In most cases, the delay wasn't the casino's fault. These small slip-ups caused the most hold-ups.

  • The account holder's name doesn't match. One of our colleagues registered his casino account without Hungarian accents, while his Revolut account had them. The casino put the request on hold and asked for a bank statement.
  • An expired document. On one test account, the ID card had expired two weeks earlier. The system accepted it at registration but not at withdrawal.
  • Proof of source of funds. For larger amounts, you may be asked for a payslip or bank statement. This happened with the 180,000 HUF request mentioned above.
  • Your deposit method doesn't support withdrawals. After depositing with Paysafecard, you'll need to choose another method, and that choice is often checked separately.
  • You've hit your limit. Anything above the daily limit is automatically moved to the next day, sometimes without any error message.
  • Manual review. A first withdrawal, a new wallet, or a big win on a particular slot can trigger a manual check. In that case, casino payouts within 24 hours are only possible if you send the requested documents straight away.
